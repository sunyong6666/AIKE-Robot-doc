# Troubleshooting and Precautions
## Card Reading Failure or Missed Reading
**Issue:** The robot does not perform the corresponding action.

**Solution:** Repeat the card reading procedure. Make sure the card is clean and flat, and do not move the card during reading. The cards must be read in the following order: **START → Command → STOP**.

## Logic Error (Missing Pair)
**Issue:** The program cannot be executed.

**Solution:** Check whether a paired card is missing.

For example:

+ **Start Loop** is present, but **End Loop** is missing.
+ **Function Start** is present, but **Function End** is missing.

## Robot Unresponsive / Frozen
**Solution:** Force-restart the robot. Press and hold all three buttons on the top of the robot simultaneously for 1 second.

**Note:** After the robot is powered off, the program stored in memory will be automatically cleared.

## Adjust the Volume
In **RFID Card Reading Mode**, power on the Aike robot.

+ Press the **A** button on the top to decrease the volume.
+ Press the **B** button on the top to increase the volume.

## Charging Precautions
+ Always use the supplied USB cable with a certified 5 V power adapter. **Recommended output: 5 V/1 A; maximum supported output: 5 V/2.5 A.**
+ **Do not use fast chargers.** Using a mobile phone fast charger may damage the device or pose more serious safety risks.

## Safety Precautions
+ **Avoid Extreme Conditions:** Keep the robot away from heat sources, open flames, and direct sunlight to prevent housing deformation or battery overheating.
+ **Handle with Care:** Do not forcefully push, pull, or drag the robot's tracks or robotic arm.
+ **Magnetic Interference:** Avoid reading RFID cards near metal objects or strong magnetic fields to prevent reading failures.
+ **Abnormal Conditions:** If you notice unusual noises or malfunctions, stop using the robot immediately.
